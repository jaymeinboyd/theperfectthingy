## Who Jayme Is

Jayme T Hunt works through **The Perfect Thingy**.

Her current public business role is:

**AI Business System Implementation**

Jayme works with small-business owners to help them make the knowledge already inside their businesses more usable.

The current service is oriented toward local small businesses in **The Dalles and surrounding Columbia Gorge communities**.

## The Core Idea

A business already contains more useful knowledge than its owner can usually see all at once.

That knowledge may live across:

- files
- email
- Google Drive
- old computers
- business software
- notebooks
- photographs
- people
- working relationships
- business history
- real numbers
- the owner's own memory
- unfinished ideas

Jayme helps owners bring the useful parts of that knowledge together so AI can work from the **business they actually have**, rather than from generic assumptions.

A central idea behind the service is:

> **Your business should be able to speak to you.**

## What the Work Looks Like

This is collaborative work, not a drop-off service.

Jayme and the business owner work together to:

1. Identify the files, knowledge, relationships, history, and unfinished ideas that matter.
2. Gather relevant information from the places where the business currently lives.
3. Establish appropriate privacy and information boundaries.
4. Build a connected, business-owned system around how that particular business actually works.
5. Test the system with real business questions.
6. Help the owner understand what the system can do, what it cannot do, and how to continue using it.

The goal is not to force every business into the same filing system or generic AI template.

The structure should reflect the actual business.

## What a Business Owner Might Use It For

Depending on the information available, a connected business system may help an owner explore questions such as:

- creating or updating a business plan from current information
- building a marketing plan around real customers and current capacity
- developing an expansion plan grounded in what already exists
- comparing equipment, staffing, software, or facility choices
- exploring succession and exit possibilities
- preparing retirement scenarios
- identifying questions to take to a lawyer, accountant, lender, financial professional, or other specialist
- finding patterns or contradictions that are difficult to see when information is scattered

The system can create working plans, comparisons, questions, and scenarios from the information the business actually contains.

It can be revised as the business changes.

## What It Does Not Do

The system does **not** make the owner's decisions.

It does **not** replace legal, tax, financial, lending, medical, or other licensed professional judgment.

It can help an owner do exploratory and preparatory work first and recognize when specialized professional advice is warranted.
