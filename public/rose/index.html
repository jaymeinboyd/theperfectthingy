Deeper recovery for accumulated business knowledge

Rose

Your business has a past. Rose helps make it usable.

Years of useful business knowledge can disappear into old folders, PDFs, notebooks, spreadsheets, drafts, emails, photographs, and the memories of the people who were there. Rose is for the part that cannot be solved by simply uploading more files.

Not just import.

Moving documents into a Vault is only the beginning. The harder work is figuring out what those materials actually say, where they agree, where they conflict, what changed over time, and what still needs a human answer.

Business memory with provenance.

Rose helps recover information without flattening uncertainty. Accepted facts can be separated from assumptions, old versions from current decisions, and source material from later interpretation.

The working sequence

Recover it. Reconcile it. Connect it.

01GatherBring together the material the business already has.
02ExtractPull out the decisions, facts, relationships, dates, obligations, and unfinished threads that matter.
03ReconcileSurface duplicates, contradictions, outdated versions, and places where the record is uncertain.
04ApproveThe owner decides what should become accepted business knowledge.
05ConnectPut the recovered knowledge where the Business Companion can actually use it.

What that can uncover

“Why did we stop doing that?”

Rose can help reconstruct the earlier decision from the records that still exist instead of quietly inventing a reason.

“Which version is actually current?”

Multiple plans, policies, price lists, grant drafts, or procedures can be compared so the business does not mistake an old document for current truth.

“What do we already know that could help with this grant?”

Accepted business evidence can be gathered into a stronger working record before it is turned into an external claim.

“What disappears if this person leaves?”

Knowledge held in one person’s files, habits, explanations, or memory can be identified before it walks out the door.

Rose does not turn ambiguity into certainty just to make the Vault look tidy.
When the record disagrees with itself, that disagreement is useful information. The owner remains the authority on what becomes accepted business truth.

Where Rose fits.

The Business Companion gives a business a usable AI-ready foundation. Rose goes deeper when the business has a backlog: years of accumulated material, inherited knowledge, scattered records, old projects, grant history, or decisions whose context has been lost.

The result is not “more files.”

The goal is a business record that is easier to question, verify, update, and carry forward—so future plans do not have to begin by reconstructing the past all over again.
