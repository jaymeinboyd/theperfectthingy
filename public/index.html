Practical capability at a human scale

Turn your knowledge and experience into business capability.

The Perfect Thingy helps individuals and small businesses turn knowledge, ideas, experience, and existing resources into practical capability. We create and teach useful tools, systems, and approaches that help people understand what they have, make better-informed decisions, preserve what they know, and build greater capacity to act for themselves.

What We Do

Business Companion A private, business-owned AI capability built around your actual business—its documents, decisions, history, priorities, and working knowledge—with a place to drop a thought, question, or unfinished idea so it can be found and connected later. Rose Recover, reconcile, and connect business knowledge already scattered across years of documents, notes, files, drafts, and systems so the past becomes usable instead of merely stored. Customer Listening Capture what customers wish you knew in their own words, preserve the meaning, and discover patterns across the responses. Business Bot A public-facing AI representative that answers from owner-approved business information instead of inventing what it does not know. 42:4U / Think Tank Preserve the working relationship with AI—and give unfinished ideas, open questions, corrections, and longer-term thinking a protected place to keep developing.

Vision

We envision communities in which individuals and small businesses can use emerging technology, practical knowledge, and creative tools without surrendering their judgment, independence, or identity. The Perfect Thingy seeks to make sophisticated capabilities accessible at a human scale—especially for people and small organizations that might otherwise be left behind by cost, complexity, geography, or rapid technological change.
